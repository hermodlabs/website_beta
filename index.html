The problem

More observations do not automatically produce more trustworthy conclusions.

Modern systems collect more data than they can confidently trust.

Distributed sensors, simulations, telemetry, and AI systems increasingly make decisions from observations collected across different locations, devices, and points in time.

Before those observations are combined, compared, or used as evidence, someone must answer a more fundamental question: Can these observations legitimately be compared?

Hermod exists to help answer that question.

What we build

Shared test and qualification infrastructure.

Rather than solving one industry's workflow, Hermod develops reusable qualification capabilities that multiple domains require. Each domain supplies its own scientific models and acceptance criteria. The infrastructure remains reusable.

How we derived the architecture

We worked backward from the jobs engineers need to accomplish.

We did not begin by assuming these domains belong together because they use sensors or distributed systems.

We analyzed the jobs test and validation personnel perform, then identified the capabilities repeatedly required across those jobs. Those shared capabilities define the platform.

The result is an architecture derived from operational evidence rather than category assumptions.
